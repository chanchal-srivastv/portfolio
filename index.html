About

Background & Philosophy

I started editing at age 10 in Filmora, cutting my first montages and steadily building a foundation in rhythm, timing, and visual craft. Today I work in DaVinci Resolve, approaching every project by visualizing the complete narrative in my head before opening the timeline—shaping each cut to serve the story and maximize viewer engagement.

Technical Perspective

Alongside editing, my background in programming, AI, and cybersecurity shapes a disciplined, systems-minded workflow—keeping projects organized, deliverables reliable, and post-production decisions intentional.
